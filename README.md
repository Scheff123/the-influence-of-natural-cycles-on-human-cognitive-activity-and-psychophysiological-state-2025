🔬 Abstract  
This study investigates the potential impact of natural cycles, particularly lunar phases, on human cognitive activity, productivity levels, and psychophysiological states. The paper explores the mechanisms underlying these effects, including how gravitational and electromagnetic fluctuations during lunar phases may influence brain function, hormonal balance, and neurotransmitter activity. The research involved a survey of 68 participants aged 18 to 50 years, who were monitored over a lunar cycle (28-30 days). Participants reported changes in cognitive performance, emotional well-being, and sleep quality, with findings suggesting heightened nervous excitability and disrupted sleep during the full moon phase, and reduced energy levels and cognitive slowdown during the new moon phase. However, individual variability was noted. The study calls for further research to better understand the physiological mechanisms at play and suggests potential applications for optimizing productivity, stress management, and sleep quality in alignment with natural biorhythms.
